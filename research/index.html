03 · Research

Questions I want to understand, not just tools I know how to run.

My interests share a common thread: models operating under structure, uncertainty and changing data conditions.

01

Mathematical foundations of learning

Probability, statistics, optimization and representation learning as tools for understanding why models behave as they do.

02

Time series & stochastic modelling

Changing distributions, multivariate temporal structure, forecasting and uncertainty in systems that evolve over time.

03

Geometric & topological learning

The role of manifolds, representations outside Euclidean space and topological structure in machine learning models.

04

Sequential decision making

Reinforcement learning, optimization under uncertainty and the design of robust recommendation and decision systems.

05

Generative modelling

Diffusion methods, structured generation and experimental evaluation of generative models across data modalities.

06

Graph & multimodal learning

Learning representations that preserve meaningful relationships across heterogeneous, graph structured and multimodal data.

Current direction

Seeking PhD opportunities in mathematically grounded machine learning.

I want to understand what structure a model learns and how that structure can be represented more faithfully. I am especially interested in research where probability, optimization, geometry, topology and graph based methods meet complex data and modern learning models.
